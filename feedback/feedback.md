# User Feedback — Quote Auto Poster

- **How collected:** Direct messages and TikTok comments
- **When:** June–July 2026

## Raw feedback

1. "The voice sounds really natural, I didn't know it was AI."
2. "I wish there were more quotes — sometimes I see the same one twice."
3. "The captions are a bit hard to read on some videos, the text is too small."
4. "Love that it's free — I was paying $30/month for a similar tool that barely worked."
5. "Can you add different languages? My audience speaks Burmese too."
6. "The background music is calming but sometimes too quiet compared to the voice."

## Themes (what keeps coming up)

- Voice quality is praised consistently
- Quote variety needs expansion (users notice repeats)
- Caption sizing/visibility needs improvement
- Users value the zero-cost approach
- Requests for multi-language support
- Audio balance between voice and music needs tuning

## Top 3 things to fix

- [ ] Increase quote pool and add smarter randomization to avoid repeats
- [ ] Improve caption font sizing — make text larger and more readable on mobile
- [ ] Adjust default audio levels — increase music volume relative to voice
